# 💪 AI Fitness Android App

🚀 A personalized fitness tracking Android application designed to eliminate confusion in the gym by automatically guiding users through structured daily workouts.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Many gym beginners struggle with:
- Not knowing which exercises to perform  
- Confusion about which muscle groups to train  
- Unstructured and inconsistent workout routines  
- Lack of progress tracking and motivation  

As a result, users follow random workouts without direction, lading to poor results and inconsistency.

---

## ✅ Solution

This application solves the problem by:

- Automatically generating daily workout plans based on:
  - User age  
  - Body weight  
  - Fitness level  

- Clearly showing:
  - What workout to do today  
  - Which muscle group to train  

- Providing a structured and guided fitness experience instead of random training.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 📅 Smart Daily Workout Plan  
- Instantly displays today's workout on app launch  
- Eliminates ecision fatigue in the gym  

### 💪 Muscle Group Focus  
- Guides users on which muscle group to train  
- Ensures balanced and effective training  

### 🔥 Streak Tracking  
- Tracks workout consistency  
- Encourages discipline and habit building  

### 📊 Weight & Progress Tracking  
- Users can log their workout weights  
- Visual graphs to track strength improvements  

### 🎯 Goal Tracking  
- Helps users meet fitness targets  
- Keeps users accountable  

### ⏱ Workout Time Monitoring  
- Tracks orkout duration  
- Helps optimize training sessions
